# Agile, Scrum & SDLC

## 1. Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC)
The process used to design, develop, and test high-quality software.
- **Phases:** Planning, Requirements Analysis, Design, Implementation (Coding), Testing, Deployment, Maintenance.
- **Waterfall Model:** A linear, sequential approach where each phase must be completed before the next begins. Highly rigid.
- **Agile Model:** An iterative approach that emphasizes continuous delivery, flexibility, and collaboration.

## 2. Scrum Framework
A subset of Agile, a lightweight framework that helps people, teams, and organizations generate value through adaptive solutions for complex problems.
- **Sprints:** Fixed-length iterations (usually 2-4 weeks) during which specific work has to be created and made ready for review.
- **Scrum Roles:**
  - *Product Owner:* Maximizes product value, manages the Product Backlog.
  - *Scrum Master:* Ensures Scrum is understood and enacted, removes impediments.
  - *Developers:* The professionals doing the work to create a usable Increment each Sprint.

## 3. Scrum Artifacts & Ceremonies
- **Artifacts:**
  - *Product Backlog:* An ordered list of everything that is known to be needed in the product.
  - *Sprint Backlog:* The set of Product Backlog items selected for the Sprint.
  - *Increment:* The sum of all the Product Backlog items completed during a Sprint.
- **Ceremonies (Events):**
  - *Sprint Planning:* Defines what can be delivered in the Sprint and how.
  - *Daily Stand-up:* A 15-minute daily meeting for the Developers to sync activities.
  - *Sprint Review:* Held at the end of the Sprint to inspect the Increment and adapt the Product Backlog.
  - *Sprint Retrospective:* An opportunity for the Scrum Team to inspect itself and create a plan for improvements to be enacted during the next Sprint.
