# Aptitude Preparation

## Percentages
Percentage means per hundred. To convert a fraction to percentage, multiply by 100. To find X percent of Y, calculate (X/100) * Y. Percentage increase = ((New - Old) / Old) * 100. Percentage decrease = ((Old - New) / Old) * 100. Shortcut: If a value increases by 20% and then decreases by 20%, it does not return to the original value. The net effect is a decrease of (20*20)/100 = 4%. If a price increases by R%, to restore the original price reduce by (R / (100 + R)) * 100 percent. If price increases by X% and consumption must decrease to keep expenditure same, decrease = (X / (100+X)) * 100. Successive percentage changes: if value changes by a% then b%, net change = a + b + (ab/100). Population formula: P_final = P_initial * (1 + r/100)^n.

## Profit and Loss
Cost Price (CP) is the price at which an item is purchased. Selling Price (SP) is the price at which it is sold. Profit = SP - CP. Loss = CP - SP. Profit percentage = (Profit / CP) * 100. Loss percentage = (Loss / CP) * 100. Marked Price (MP) is the listed price before discount. Discount = MP - SP. Discount percentage = (Discount / MP) * 100. SP = MP * (1 - discount/100). SP = CP * (1 + profit/100). Successive discounts of 20% and 10%: effective discount = 20 + 10 - (20*10)/100 = 28%. If a shopkeeper cheats by using false weights, gain% = (true weight - false weight) / false weight * 100. Break-even: when total revenue equals total cost, profit is zero.

## Simple and Compound Interest
Simple Interest = (P * R * T) / 100. Amount = P + SI. Compound Interest: Amount = P * (1 + R/100)^T. CI = Amount - P. Key difference: SI calculates interest on the original principal only, CI calculates interest on principal plus accumulated interest. For two years, difference between CI and SI equals P * (R/100)^2. For three years, difference = P * (R/100)^2 * (3 + R/100). Rule of 72: divide 72 by the interest rate to estimate years to double money. At 12%, money doubles in ~6 years. When interest is compounded half-yearly: rate becomes R/2, time becomes 2T. Quarterly: rate R/4, time 4T. Effective rate when compounded n times: (1 + R/(100n))^n - 1.

## Ratio and Proportion
A ratio compares two quantities. If A:B = 3:4, then A is 3 parts and B is 4 parts out of 7 total parts. A = (3/7) * Total, B = (4/7) * Total. Proportion means two ratios are equal: A/B = C/D, then AD = BC. Direct proportion: if one increases, other increases in same ratio. Inverse proportion: if one increases, other decreases. If three quantities are in continued proportion a:b = b:c, then b² = ac. Componendo and Dividendo: if a/b = c/d, then (a+b)/(a-b) = (c+d)/(c-d). Mixtures and alligation: if two items priced at C1 and C2 are mixed to get mean price Cm, mixing ratio = (C2-Cm)/(Cm-C1). Replacement formula: after n replacements of x litres from container of V litres, quantity of original = V * (1 - x/V)^n.

## Time Speed and Distance
Distance = Speed * Time. Speed = Distance / Time. Time = Distance / Speed. Convert km/h to m/s: multiply by 5/18. Convert m/s to km/h: multiply by 18/5. Average speed for two equal distances at speeds S1 and S2: 2*S1*S2/(S1+S2), not the arithmetic mean. Relative speed: same direction subtract speeds, opposite direction add speeds. For trains: time to cross a pole = length of train / speed. Time to cross a platform = (length of train + length of platform) / speed. Two trains crossing each other (opposite direction): time = (L1+L2) / (S1+S2). Same direction: time = (L1+L2) / |S1-S2|. Boats and streams: downstream speed = boat speed + stream speed. Upstream speed = boat speed - stream speed. Boat speed = (downstream + upstream) / 2. Stream speed = (downstream - upstream) / 2.

## Time and Work
If A can complete a job in X days, A's daily work rate is 1/X. If A and B work together, combined rate is 1/X + 1/Y, and time to finish is XY/(X+Y). If A is twice as efficient as B, A's rate is double B's rate. LCM method: take LCM of individual times as total work units, calculate units per day for each worker. Pipes and cisterns: inlet pipe fills a tank, outlet pipe empties it. If an inlet fills in X hours and outlet empties in Y hours (Y > X for net fill), net rate is 1/X - 1/Y. If A works for some days and then B joins, calculate work done by A alone, remaining work done together. Work equivalence: M1 * D1 * H1 / W1 = M2 * D2 * H2 / W2 (men * days * hours / work). If 5 men can do a job in 10 days, 10 men can do it in 5 days (assuming equal efficiency).

## Averages
Average = Sum of values / Number of values. If the average of N numbers is A, the sum is N * A. When a new number is added, new average = (old sum + new number) / (N + 1). Weighted average = sum of (value * weight) / sum of weights. If the average increases by X when a new element is added, the new element = old average + X * (N + 1). If one number is replaced, new average = old average + (new value - old value) / N. Cricket average: if a batsman's average after N innings is A, and he scores R in (N+1)th innings, new average = (N*A + R) / (N+1). For the average to increase by X, runs needed = current average + X * (N+1). Median: middle value in sorted data. Mode: most frequent value.

## Probability
Probability = Number of favourable outcomes / Total number of outcomes. Probability ranges from 0 (impossible) to 1 (certain). P(A') = 1 - P(A) where A' is complement of A. For independent events, P(A and B) = P(A) * P(B). For mutually exclusive events, P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B). For non-mutually exclusive events, P(A or B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A and B). Conditional probability: P(A|B) = P(A and B) / P(B). Bayes theorem: P(A|B) = P(B|A) * P(A) / P(B). Playing cards: 52 cards, 4 suits (hearts, diamonds, clubs, spades), 13 per suit, 4 aces, 12 face cards. Dice: 6 faces, P(any face) = 1/6, two dice total outcomes = 36. Coins: P(head) = 1/2, n coins total outcomes = 2^n. At least one: P(at least 1) = 1 - P(none). Exactly one: use inclusion-exclusion.

## Permutations and Combinations
Permutation is arrangement where order matters. nPr = n! / (n-r)!. Combination is selection where order does not matter. nCr = n! / (r! * (n-r)!). nCr = nC(n-r). nC0 = nCn = 1. Total permutations of n distinct objects = n!. Permutations with repetition: if an object repeats k times, divide by k!. Example: letters in MISSISSIPPI = 11! / (4! * 4! * 2!). Circular permutations of n objects = (n-1)!. With necklace (flip matters): (n-1)!/2. Key insight: if the question asks "how many ways to arrange," use permutation. If it asks "how many ways to choose or select," use combination. Combinations with repetition: (n+r-1)Cr. Distribute r identical objects into n distinct groups: (n+r-1)C(r). Committee formation: choose k members from n people = nCk. With at least one condition: total - complement.

## Number Systems
Divisibility rules: by 2 if last digit is even, by 3 if digit sum is divisible by 3, by 4 if last two digits form a number divisible by 4, by 5 if last digit is 0 or 5, by 6 if divisible by both 2 and 3, by 8 if last three digits divisible by 8, by 9 if digit sum divisible by 9, by 11 if alternating sum of digits divisible by 11. HCF (GCD): largest common factor, find using prime factorisation or Euclidean algorithm. LCM: smallest common multiple. HCF * LCM = Product of two numbers. Remainder theorem: remainder of (a*b)/n = ((a%n) * (b%n)) % n. Euler's theorem: a^phi(n) mod n = 1 when gcd(a,n)=1. Power cycles: last digit of a^n repeats in cycles (2: cycle 2,4,8,6; 3: cycle 3,9,7,1). Number of factors: if N = p1^a * p2^b, factors = (a+1)(b+1). Sum of factors = (p1^0 + p1^1 + ... + p1^a) * (p2^0 + ... + p2^b).

## Logical Reasoning
Blood relations: understand family tree terminology (paternal, maternal, sibling, cousin, nephew, niece). Draw family tree step by step. Coding-decoding: identify the pattern in how letters or numbers are transformed. Letter shift (+1, +2, reverse), number coding, mixed coding. Seating arrangement: use diagrams for linear and circular arrangements. Track directions (left/right), fixed positions, relative positions. Direction sense: draw a compass (N/S/E/W) and trace the path step by step. Right turn clockwise, left turn anticlockwise. Calculate net displacement using Pythagoras. Syllogisms: use Venn diagrams. All A are B means A is completely inside B. Some A are B means overlap exists. No A are B means no overlap. Conclusions: "All A are B" does NOT mean "All B are A." "Some A are B" means "Some B are A" is also true.

## Data Interpretation
Tables: read row and column headers carefully, identify what each cell represents. Calculate percentage change, ratio, average from given data. Bar charts: compare heights or lengths of bars, calculate differences and ratios. Grouped bars compare categories. Stacked bars show composition. Pie charts: each slice represents a percentage of the total. Convert percentages to actual values: value = percentage * total / 100. Central angle = percentage * 360 / 100. Line graphs: identify trends (increasing, decreasing, constant), calculate rate of change, compare multiple lines. Mixed graphs: combine bar and line or table and pie chart. Caselets: data given in paragraph form, extract numbers and solve. For all types: read the question carefully, identify what data you need, perform calculations step by step, verify answer makes sense, manage time (do not spend too long on one question).

## Clocks and Calendars
Clock: minute hand moves 6 degrees per minute. Hour hand moves 0.5 degrees per minute. Relative speed = 5.5 degrees per minute. Angle between hands = |30H - 5.5M| where H is hour, M is minutes. If angle > 180, take 360 - angle. Hands overlap 11 times in 12 hours (every 65.45 minutes). Hands are at right angle (90°) 22 times in 12 hours. Hands are opposite (180°) 11 times in 12 hours. Calendar: odd days method. 1 ordinary year = 1 odd day. 1 leap year = 2 odd days. 100 years = 5 odd days. 200 years = 3 odd days. 300 years = 1 odd day. 400 years = 0 odd days. Leap year: divisible by 4, century years divisible by 400. Day codes: Sunday=0, Monday=1, etc. Find day by counting total odd days from a reference date.
